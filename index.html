Information and consent to participate

Please read this information before deciding whether to participate. Keep a copy of this page if you want a record of the information provided.

Study purpose: This study examines how performance changes when Tetris is performed alone or alongside a visual mental-rotation or auditory sound-discrimination task.

What you will do: Answer two brief eligibility questions and a demographic survey; practice Tetris, phoneme discrimination, and mental rotation for 1.5 minutes each; then complete five three-minute test blocks, some with two tasks at once. After each test block, you will answer six brief workload questions about that block. You will respond with the keyboard and hear sounds through headphones. The session takes about 20–30 minutes, including setup and instructions.

Eligibility: You must be 18 or older, have normal or corrected-to-normal vision (with glasses or contact lenses if needed), and report typical hearing without hearing aids. You must also be able to use a keyboard for the tasks. After consenting, you will answer two required eligibility questions; if you do not meet both criteria, or are unsure, the session will end before the demographic survey or tasks. Eligibility is based on your answers, not a clinical test. Do not use audio at an uncomfortable volume.

Risks and benefits: You may experience minor fatigue, frustration, or eye/ear discomfort. You may stop at any time; there is no direct benefit promised. Keep volume moderate, take breaks between blocks if needed, and stop if uncomfortable.

Voluntary participation: Participation is optional. You may decline or stop without penalty. Before sharing the downloaded CSV, you can close this tab and delete the file to withhold your results. If you have already shared the file, contact the researcher with your participant ID to ask whether your data can still be withdrawn; removal may not be possible after data have been combined or de-identified.

Data and privacy: This page does not ask for your name. For participants who meet the eligibility criteria, it records your participant ID, eligibility answers, survey responses, task performance, and timestamps in a CSV downloaded to this device; ineligible participants’ answers are not downloaded or stored by this page; the page itself does not upload those data. If automatic counterbalancing is used, this browser stores your participant ID and sequence assignment locally. The CSV is not anonymous to anyone who can connect your participant ID to you; anyone who receives the file can read its contents. Do not put your name or email address in the participant ID or optional text answers. This page does not specify how a researcher will store, share, or retain a CSV you send them. Ask the researcher about those arrangements before sharing it, and do not participate until you have the information you need to decide.

Compensation: This website does not provide payment or course credit. If you were offered either separately, ask the researcher about the amount, eligibility, and what happens if you stop early before deciding whether to participate.
